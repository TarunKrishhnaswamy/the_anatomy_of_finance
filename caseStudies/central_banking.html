Case Study: Do We Even Need Central Banks?

Home Central Banks

🔍 Overview

This case study explores the historical and modern roles of central banks, drawing insights from Zerodha Varsity’s article. It contrasts centralized monetary control with free banking systems like 18th-century Scotland.

📊 Dashboard Summary

  • Central Banks: Inflation control, financial oversight, lender of last resort
  • Criticism: Linked to major crises (1929, 1970s, 2008)
  • Free Banking: Market discipline, gold-backed notes, competitive redemption
  • Key Concepts: Fractional reserves, moral hazard, Overton Window

🌍 Historical Evidence

Over 60 cases of free banking across Canada, Australia, France, and the US. Milton Friedman argued markets could self-regulate without central banks.

🧭 Why Central Banks Persist

Political motives and modern complexity (inequality, housing, climate risk) make centralized oversight necessary.

🧠 Reflection

This case study encourages rethinking assumptions about monetary policy and exploring ideas beyond conventional discourse.
